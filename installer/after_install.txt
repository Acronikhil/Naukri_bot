Naukri Profile Bot is installed. Finish setup in 4 steps:

1. LOG IN
   Open the app. In "Naukri account", click "Log in with browser" and sign in to
   Naukri (Google, OTP or password). Or choose "Email & Password" and click
   "Save credentials".

2. CHOOSE TIMES
   In "Schedule", add the times you want your profile refreshed every day
   (for example 10:00 AM, 12:30 PM, 2:00 PM) and pick a "Run until" date.

3. ALLOW WAKE FROM SLEEP
   In "Status", if "Wake from sleep" says Disabled, click "Enable" so updates
   also run while your laptop is asleep.

4. ACTIVATE
   Click "Activate schedule". Use "Update now" any time to test it.

That's it. You can close the app: updates keep running in the background,
even when your laptop is locked or asleep, as long as you stay signed in
to Windows. If your Naukri login expires, a browser window opens and asks
you to log in again.

Requirements: Windows 10 or 11 with Microsoft Edge (pre-installed on Windows).
